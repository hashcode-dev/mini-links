Mini-Links | URL Shortener, Branded Short Links & Analytics

Mini-Links is an enterprise-grade link management platform providing instantaneous sub-10ms URL shortening, dynamic QR code generation, click geolocation analytics, and custom domain branding.

Step-by-Step Guide: How to Shorten Links

  1. Paste any destination link into the input field above.
  2. Optionally enter a custom branded alias (e.g. minilinks.com/my-brand).
  3. Click Shorten Link to generate your shortened URL and instant vector QR code.
